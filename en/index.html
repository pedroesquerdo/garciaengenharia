About us

Engineering and architecture for everyone, with quality at every stage.

Founded in 2020 by engineer Lucas Garcia, Garcia Engineering & Construction was created to make engineering and architecture more accessible without compromising technical quality, innovation or attention to every decision.

From design to construction, we turn complex requirements into clear, feasible solutions and oversee every stage so clients can build with greater confidence and peace of mind.

What services does Garcia provide?

Architectural and engineering design, accessibility, regularization, documentation, construction and technical oversight.

Can I hire Garcia for design only?

Yes. The scope may cover design and documentation only, technical oversight or complete construction, depending on your needs.

How does the quote process work?

During the first contact, we identify the property type, location, required service and current stage. Garcia then defines the scope and presents a proposal.

Does Garcia handle permits and regularization?

Yes. The company handles designs, permits, occupancy certificates and municipal regularization procedures according to each case.

What area do you serve?

Garcia is based in São José do Rio Preto. Availability in nearby cities is confirmed during the initial consultation.
